    03 / A more useful demo day

    Make the room better at the problem.

    A proposed event format that rewards useful contribution and tested combinations. The sponsor keeps the approved shared work.

    ROUND 01

    Establish a baseline

    Give teams the same scoped problem and assistance entitlement. Record outcomes, effort, costs and unresolved evidence.

    ROUND 02

    Combine strengths

    Let teams exchange approved contributions. Test whether the combination improves the accepted result.

    ROUND 03

    Share what worked

    Credit a useful adapter, method or correction. Have another team test it on fresh material before adopting it.

    ROUND 04

    Prove continuity

    Change a premise. Move the work. Replace a component, including AXM. Measure what continues and what remains.

    04 / Keep control contestable

    Useful collaboration.
    Continuing choice.

    The Clifford Number research asks how interests become infrastructure and how infrastructure becomes power. Here, that question becomes an operating design: the problem, evidence, rules and continuing work remain inspectable.

    Explore Clifford Number ↗AXM Readiness ↗
    1. 01

      The sponsor owns the problem and acceptance

      Version the criteria, retain decisions and make exceptions visible. A tool supplier does not acquire the right to choose its rivals.

    2. 02

      Participants choose what they share

      Explicit contributions preserve credit and scope. Private work, model weights and unrelated records stay outside the exchange.

    3. 03

      Capability travels with its evidence

      Retain inputs, mappings, conclusions and corrections so another operator can inspect and continue the work.

    4. 04

      MARY and AXM are replaceable too

      A compatible workbench may implement the same contract. Continued participation should depend on useful performance.

    Release boundaries and provenance

    Team Commons 0.2.0. Deterministic browser application with no model calls, account, analytics or upload endpoint. The three roles are synthetic, not a list of real bidders. A local operator label is not an authenticated authorization. Hashes are unsigned integrity identifiers. A field-mapping success covers this example only. Copying a browser workspace is not proof of secure multi-tenant deployment or production workflow substitution.

    This public release reuses the tested cooperative-exchange engine. It does not redistribute the separately licensed MARY Portable core, private estate artifacts or a conversational model. The original portable-core fixture checks remain separate from the browser product's acceptance.
