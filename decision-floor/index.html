CONTRACT-SCOPED EVIDENCE EXCHANGE

Two companies.
Two recorders.
One measurable disagreement.

Each organization keeps its source vault and private signing key. A counterpart receives only fields its own contract admits, verifies the signed statement against a pinned public key, and compares it with independent observations. A contradiction creates a human review, not an automatic rewrite.
