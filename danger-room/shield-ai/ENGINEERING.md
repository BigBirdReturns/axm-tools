# From customer requirement to repeatable product evidence

This module extends the existing Danger Room handoff at `../index.html`. Its native replay and camera formats retain their original receipt and source identity. The workbench adds a complete commercial-to-engineering operating path; it keeps the recorded native demonstration, new browser execution and proposed external product integration distinguishable.

## 1. Agree the customer decision

Record the customer outcome, buying process, product scope, owner, acceptance evaluator and required evidence. A product claim is the conjunction of a defined behavior, conditions under which it applies, a measured result and an owner who can support the delivery. The same record supplies the pursuit brief, engineering requirements, demonstration scope and customer handoff.

## 2. Bind the interface

Preserve original logs. Name the version, units, coordinate conventions, source and receipt clocks, message ordering, resets, observation availability and accepted actions. Camera intake in this application analyzes the existing proposed frame format. It calculates queue delay within the Pi monotonic clock domain, identifies absent entries and arrival reordering, and preserves uncertainty about where a loss occurred. It never derives camera-to-Pi latency without a measured clock mapping.

## 3. Reproduce the decision-relevant failure

The original native receipt records the Fly-0 observation-loss experiment, controller rerun equality, loopback-TCP action equality and a tamper rejection. The complete original ledgers were temporary; replaying the embedded samples preserves that evidence boundary. The October 27 native audit is a separate run set, with eight valid ledgers and no admitted candidate.

The browser reference harness adds an executable, seeded industrial 2D measurement-quality example. It has ideal commanded actuation, a known smooth path, noisy measurements and explicit delayed, missing or biased observations. It is useful for exercising the requirement-to-release mechanism. It does not establish physical fidelity or integration with any Shield AI product.

## 4. Evaluate and retain an improvement

The reactive baseline consumes the stale measurement. The supported candidate predicts commanded displacement from the measurement timestamp and stops when support expires. The fixed predicates test peak, RMS and terminal error, complete finite records and measurement age. Fresh seed evaluation retains every result. The timing-only scope covers nominal operation, dropout and delay; expanding the same requirement to bias exposes the candidate's remaining failure. A scoped success never silently becomes the broader claim.

## 5. Package and operate the accepted scope

The quality result flows into the product definition, claim, customer acceptance plan, partner workshare and release/handoff. Procurement evidence and buyer acceptance remain independent commercial conditions. A technical success can remove its own blocker while an unresolved budget, buyer or procurement decision still holds the forecast. The operating review computes its figures from the current opportunity records, counts duplicates once, and keeps pipeline, bookings and recognized revenue distinct.

## 6. Continue engineering through customer feedback

New evidence creates a new requirement or regression case with an owner. Integration mismatches, missing observations, changed conditions and failed acceptance retain their evidence. The native AXM frontier documented on October 1 still includes estimator support, range and gate identity, sparse telemetry and reacquisition; the successful simple-double results and selected-frame perception scores remain separate from an end-to-end capture-profile result.

## Interface to an actual organization

The browser workbench supports local record entry, file intake, workspace import/export and artifact generation. The organization supplies its account records, owners, product interfaces, commercial authorities and source evidence. Connecting CRM, identity, customer records, model services, simulation backends or outbound communications is explicit integration work. These connections can use the same object and evidence contracts without changing the customer-facing decision model.
