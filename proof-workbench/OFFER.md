# Buyer-owned reproducibility evaluation

## Deliverable

One consequential decision, one agreed source mapping, one versioned acceptance protocol, original and corrected evidence, retained failures, an executable local source kit, a clean-recipient replay, and a written disposition against the agreed criteria. The buyer retains inputs, outputs, verification machinery and a usable handover. AXM itself remains replaceable.

## Entry conditions

The receiving decision owner and data custodian identify permitted data, disclosure, acceptance, operational boundaries and the budget holder. Public-reference demonstrations and signed source manifests do not supply customer permission. Scope preparation is free of external effects. Real customer execution starts only under separately recorded authority.

## Pricing and capacity

Use the workbench's delivery-economics calculation with your actual engineering estimate, internal hourly cost, compute allowance and support reserve. It exposes estimated contribution and refuses invalid figures. The prior chat's price bands were hypotheses, not established prices, customer agreements or market evidence. Freeze actual price, payment schedule, scope, acceptance and change treatment separately for each engagement.

## Acceptance and exit

Freeze the acceptance criteria before execution. Preserve failures and missing evidence. Give a recipient the source kit and an independently authenticated digest. Recipient test and acceptance are separate recorded acts. Agree who owns fixes, retests and support duration. End with accepted-for-scope, failed-with-reason, inconclusive-with-missing-evidence, or withdrawn-with-history. No automatic subscription, new payment arrangement, hidden dependency or claim of ROI is introduced.

## Private engagement record

`python -B opportunity.py /private/opportunity.json --output /private/new-state.json` projects evidence-referenced correspondence into explicit stages. Auto-replies, intermediary follow-up and reported attention do not advance the technical stage. Courtesy access does not become audit permission. Acceptance requires a prior agreed test and a recipient execution reference. The reducer checks structure; references must be substantiated by their actual sources. No email, scheduling or customer contact is performed.
