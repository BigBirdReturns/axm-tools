Subject: Update Regarding Proposed Protected Tree Removal at Santa Anita Park

Thank you for taking the time to contact the City and for sharing your comments and concerns regarding the proposed LA28 cross-country course and the potential removal of protected trees at Santa Anita Park. We recognize the importance of this issue to many community members and appreciate your participation in the process.

At the request of Santa Anita Park, the Applicant, processing of protected tree removal permit TRH 26-11/TRE 26-09 has been placed on hold. Santa Anita Park has shared that work is already underway with the International Equestrian Federation (FEI) course design team, LA28, and construction engineers to further evaluate opportunities to address potential impacts to protected trees while preparing for the 2028 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

Since review of the permit application has been paused, the City's administrative review process is also on hold. Currently, there is no decision regarding the proposed tree removals pending before City staff or the Arcadia City Council. The City will continue to follow all review and public notification processes should a revised proposal be submitted. If a revised permit application is submitted, the public notification process will begin again to allow the community to review and provide input on the revised plans. Updates and relevant information will be made available on the project webpage, where interested parties can subscribe to project updates:  www.ArcadiaCA.gov/oaktreesatccc<http://www.ArcadiaCA.gov/oaktreesatccc>.


We appreciate every community member who has taken the time to share their perspective. Arcadia remains committed to keeping the community informed as additional information becomes available and ensuring any future proposal follows the applicable review and notification process.

City's press release - https://www.arcadiaca.gov/news/?rz=newsDetails&id=429

Thank you for your continued participation and engagement.
