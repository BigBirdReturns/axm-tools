Re: California Public Records Act Request - TRH 26-11, TRE 26-09, and the LA28 Eventing Cross-Country 
Course at Santa Anita Park
Pursuant to the California Public Records Act, Government Code section 7920.000 et seq., I request electronic copies of the 
records described below concerning Protected Healthy Tree Removal Permit TRH 26-11, Protected Tree Encroachment Permit 
TRE 26-09, and the proposed LA28 eventing cross-country course at Santa Anita Park. The requested period is 1 January 2025 
through the date of the City's search unless a category expressly requires an earlier record to explain a current plan, agreement, 
asset, or decision.
This request covers records created, received, transmitted, possessed, controlled, or maintained by the City, including records 
held for the City by consultants or contractors. It includes writings in every form recognized by the Act, including emails and 
attachments, text or messaging records used for City business, memoranda, notes, meeting records, presentations, spreadsheets, 
databases, geospatial files, CAD or plan files, photographs, videos, contracts, draft and final reports, native electronic files, and 
metadata ordinarily maintained with those records.
Please produce every version of the cross-country course map, route, alignment, site plan, grading plan, drainage plan, obstacle 
plan, access plan, operational plan, construction plan, tree-impact plan, tree-protection plan, and restoration plan. This category 
includes rejected, superseded, preliminary, annotated, redlined, and presentation versions, as well as records showing when, by 
whom, and why each version was changed.
Please produce every alternatives analysis, alternatives matrix, design narrative, feasibility study, comparison, decision log, cost 
or schedule comparison, tree-impact count, presentation, meeting record, or communication that evaluates or rejects a materially 
different course design. This category includes every record concerning a zero-removal, no-removal, tree-exclusion, hardscape-
first, parking-lot, infield, track, turf, temporary-overlay, or other lower-impact route, whether or not the exact terminology was 
used.
Please produce every record identifying the mandatory technical criteria applied to route selection or tree conflicts, including 
FEI or Olympic sporting requirements; horse and rider welfare criteria; course length, speed, grade, turning radius, obstacle, 
sightline, footing, surface-transition, warm-up, veterinary, emergency, fire, evacuation, accessibility, security, broadcast, 
spectator, stabling, circulation, grading, drainage, utility, construction, and restoration requirements; and any distinction 
between mandatory requirements and preferences relating to cost, schedule, aesthetics, broadcast, hospitality, spectator experience, 
construction convenience, or post-Games use.
Please produce every tree-specific record for the 34 proposed removals and 45 proposed protected-zone encroachments, 
including survey data, species, trunk diameter, age estimate, condition, protected-zone geometry, canopy, known root structure, 
photographs, arborist analysis, biological analysis, grading or utility conflict, proposed work inside the protected zone, attempted 
redesign, mitigation, monitoring, and the technical requirement asserted to remain after redesign. Please include all structured or 
geospatial source data used to generate tree maps or impact counts.
Please produce every record concerning the completeness, processing, notice, and decision schedule for TRH 26-11 and TRE 26-
09, including the application, all supplements, completeness correspondence, the date each application was deemed complete, 
notices of pending decision, mailing lists and declarations of mailing, public comments, staff analysis, consultant review, draft 
findings, conditions, proposed decisions, environmental-review status, and appeal instructions. Please include records concerning 
the August 27, 2026 community meeting and any decision to modify, maintain, withdraw, or resubmit either application after 
that meeting.
Please produce every record concerning the City project webpage and its technical links, including the posting, migration, 
storage, removal, failure, repair, testing, or replacement of the linked course map, arborist assessment, biological resources
