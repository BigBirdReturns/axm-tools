NOTICE OF PENDING DECISION IS HEREBY GIVEN that Santa Anita Park at 285 
W. Huntington Drive is requesting to remove protected trees to accommodate a new
equestrian cross-country course for the Los Angeles LA 28 Olympics, as described
below. You have the opportunity to comment on the project described below prior to
the City making a decision.
A.
Exempt from the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) pursuant to
the following Sections of the CEQA Guidelines: Section 15301 (Class 1),
Section 15323 (Class 23), and Section 15332 (Class 32).
B.
Protected Healthy Tree Removal Permit No. TRH 26-11 and Protected Tree
Encroachment Permit No. TRE 26-09
Project Description: Santa Anita Park is requesting approval to remove 33 Coast 
Live Oak trees and one (1) Engelmann Oak tree, and to encroach into the protected 
zone of 45 protected trees to accommodate construction of a new equestrian cross-
country course for the Los Angeles LA 2028 Olympics Games. The proposed course 
is located within the hillside area adjacent to the existing north parking lot and the 
Lower Rancho residential neighborhood along Stanford Drive. The course has been 
designed to maximize the preservation of mature trees where feasible. The Applicant 
is proposing 34 replacement oak trees at the racetrack property or other public facing 
locations.  
Comment Period:  August 4, 2026 to August 17, 2026 at 5:30 PM
