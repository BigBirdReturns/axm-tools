Olympics and Paralympics Work (FINAL) 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The purpose of this memo is to identify responsible staff from the Development Services 
Department for specific aspects of planning and implementation work related to the 2028 
Olympics and Paralympics. As you all know, there have been numerous LA28 Olympic 
“summits”, “gatherings”, informational sessions, and other hype sessions that many of us 
have attended. Some are more focused than others, some are topical, most are still at 
the 30,000 foot level. We have been doing our best over the past year or so to have the 
right people attending the right sessions, where value can be added. As we are now 
getting closer to having an actual plan for Santa Anita Park, it is important to figure out 
who should be doing what and how these items work together. Clearly, this will evolve 
over time, and duties and focus will shift, but once the Olympic behemoth starts rolling 
along, I just want us to be ready for it locally so we are not scrambling.   
I’ve organized this into the most logical topical areas as I see them. I have also attached 
a previously-written memo talking about zoning, entitlements, and construction issues, 
which will be a large part of our upcoming work.  
Contracts and Agreements (All) 
There will be a series of agreements between LA28 and related entities. I have worked 
on the initial Venue City Agreement with Dominic, Justine, and Mike. This was approved 
by the City Council in October. There will be important subsequent agreements that will 
largely be handled by the City Manager’s office related to division of resources and 
responsibilities, reimbursement, and other key issues. There may be aspects of these 
agreements that will be pushed to DSD, but I believe the CM office will have primary

Games Footprint and “Blast Radius” (Kevin) 
This is ongoing and an extremely important part of all of the subsequent work we will do. 
This is the official area that is designated as the Games footprint, where all competitions, 
viewing, and official Olympic activities will occur. As you all know, there is no parking or 
general access within this area. As such, the LA28 Security teams are developing the 
“blast radius” for the footprint, which essentially will determine the danger zone for 
possible threats or attacks on the footprint area. The designation of the blast radius is of 
major importance as it will determine which streets will be closed, which areas will need 
lockdown, etc. For example, if Colorado Place or even Baldwin Ave are included, that will 
be a significant impact on city functions. We should know these designations soon, and 
while I think they will be final pretty soon, they will likely change and evolve based on 
expected impacts.  
Kevin has been the most involved in this as the discussion of transportation into the venue 
have been critical to this portion of the work. Once this information is released, Kevin 
would likely be the lead on getting this out to you all.  
Zoning, Approvals, and Construction (Lisa and Ken, John on Engineering issues) 
As mentioned above, see the attached memo on this that was prepared a couple of 
months ago. This memo was circulated to Dominic, Mike, and Lisa and we all agreed on 
the process. It is important to note that we are treating this as an actual land use and not 
a temporary use permit or special event. Clearly, with something as big as the Olympics, 
with all the various players involved, the work on these aspects will be very reactionary. 
Meaning, we will likely get plans late and be asked for immediate approvals, plans will 
change frequently, and asks will come quickly. Just like everything we always deal with at 
the Racetrack.  
Lisa and Ken will be the primary contacts for this part of the work. The representative I 
have been working with from the track is Nici Boon, who many of you know. I spoke with 
Nici on November 21. She is finishing up the due diligence phase and shared with me the 
broad strokes of a schedule moving forward. They have submitted their “final” course 
design to LA28 and believe they will have approval soon. Nici is looking to schedule an 
“all hands” meeting between Santa Anita, her team, and the City, in mid-December. She 
is going to wrangle her people and get me an email suggesting a date or dates. This will 
be an important meeting as it will set the schedule and roles moving forward.    
Nici will be submitting a tree removal plan and tree pruning plan at some point soon as 
well. We will need to send this out to the neighbors as well and notice it.  
Another aspect of Zoning that we should consider is what to do about short-term rentals. 
As you all know, we don’t allow rentals of less than 28 days. We may want to consider

Advertising and Marketing (Simon) 
One aspect of the LA28 workforce is a group that deals with local advertising and 
marketing. Rather than just focusing on Olympic themed banners and/or Olympic 
imagery, they also sell marketing rights to their major partners. We have been asked to 
provide our marketing assets and options which are currently under review by Jillian Scott 
of LA28. Justine is aware of this work, and will likely be the lead, but I will also send the 
information to Simon to be involved with this one because it has to do with Economic 
Development and marketing. Essentially, LA28 “auctions” a City’s assets (e.g. banner 
poles, installation locations, across the street banners, etc.) as part of a package to their 
major sponsors. I have no idea if our assets will be of interest but maybe to sponsors of 
equestrian things….like Longines, Blancpain, and the like. In any event, Jillian Scott has 
our spreadsheet and supporting documentation and they are reviewing it.  
At some point we will need to take an item to City Council if we are to use our assets for 
something other than non-profit and community events. This could likely be a Resolution 
or simply approval to allow Olympic related advertising as an exception to our policy.  
Transit and Transportation (Kevin and Alana. John backing up) 
This is probably the piece that we are furthest along on. Kevin and Alana (with John as 
backup) have attended several different recurring meetings, including the Games Route 
Network meetings, First Mile/Last Mile, and various meetings hosted by Metro or Caltrans 
or even SCAG. There are a lot of cooks in the kitchen on these issues, and they range 
from the regional network of bus lines and remote parking areas to very site specific 
methods of getting people into Santa Anita Park from the light rail station and beyond. 
This will be the focus of a lot of our work.  
While we do know some of the regional stuff, the games footprint and blast radius stuff 
will start to frame out how Arcadia gets people into the venue. So, that is a key upcoming 
step as mentioned. We will need to tier off of that to determine pathways into the venue, 
routes for buses and shuttles, and other methods of moving people. Arcadia Transit will 
likely have a large role, as will additional shuttle services and official Olympic transport.  
One of the most interesting aspects of this work is how we are going to make it a good 
user experience to get from the A Line Station to the venue. We have talked about options 
for closing Santa Clara Street and making it a “games experience” street. This could be 
a very cool option. We also need to focus on how we get people from the corner of 
Huntington and Colorado Place next to the Hilton, into the site. This will likely need to 
involve closures or new walkways and will need to involve private investment on the SART 
site as well because there is no user-friendly path once you leave the public right of way 
into the venue. An option we have discussed is using grant money or CDBG money to 
create an ADA-accessible path to the venue. The time is right to start talking about this.

Related to this is something that is coming through the first mile/last mile efforts called 
the Olympic “Kit of Parts”. In simple terms, this is a set of items that local venue cities can 
complete that enhance the visitor experience. Examples might be pedestrian amenities, 
shade structures, hydration stations, sidewalk improvements, etc. This will be something 
that might end up being better aligned with the “involving the community” section below, 
but it is a separate and distinct group from LA28.  
Involving the Community and Businesses, Viewing Parties, etc. (Simon, All) 
Dominic has recently started a team of internal folks to begin talking about this. What do 
we do ourselves to make the City ready for a great Olympic experience?  Marketing feeds 
into this, as well as connections with the Chamber of Commerce and other local business 
groups or maybe major players in town like the Shops at Santa Anita, USC Arcadia, 
AUSD, etc. What can we do in the A-Line Plaza that feels like a permanent nod to the 
Games? Viewing parties in town…where and how? Also, are there local efforts to create 
imagery and installations welcoming the Games?  
In terms of the Chamber and the business connections, everyone wants to be involved in 
some way. There may be low cost but effective ways to help businesses attract fans, 
promote the games, etc. Another aspect that should be kicked off fairly soon.  
Finally, there are likely to be businesses, private parties, Shops at Santa Anita, etc. that 
will want to host their own events and/or watch parties or promotions related to the 
Olympics. I have heard, too, that Santa Anita Park may set up a daily “watch party” at the 
venue as well. We should be giving some thought to our TUP process and allowing a 
certain streamlined level of review for these types of events around the Games. They will 
likely vary widely in magnitude so a collective approach makes sense. This aligns with 
the comments earlier about changing the regulations for use of banners and assets as 
well as the comments on short term rentals. There likely is a suite of regulations we should 
loosen for the Olympics and we can probably do this through City Council Resolution. We 
did this for certain things during COVID, like outdoor dining, and a similar process could 
be completed here.
