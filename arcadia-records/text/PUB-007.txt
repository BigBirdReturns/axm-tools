COMMUNITY MEETING 
  Removal of Oak Trees for LA28 Cross Country Course 
In partnership with Santa Anita Park and the LA28 Team, the City is providing the community with an 
opportunity to learn more about the proposed removal of 34 protected trees associated with the LA28 Cross 
Country Course at Santa Anita Park and the proposed planting of 34 new trees. A Community Meeting will be 
held on Thursday, August 27, 2026, from 5:00–6:30 PM at the Chandelier Room at Santa Anita Park.  
 
This meeting will provide an opportunity to share the proposed project with a broader community, as 
comments and questions have been received from residents beyond the Arcadia area. No decision will be 
made prior to the August 27, 2026, Community Meeting. 
 
An overview of the proposed LA 28 Cross Country Course, including the proposed course design and site 
improvements: 
 
• 
Information regarding the 34 protected oak trees proposed for removal, the trees that will be retained 
within the hillside area, the trees that will be encroached upon, and the planting of 34 new replacement 
trees. 
• 
An opportunity for community members to view the proposed project and ask questions directly of project 
representatives. 
• 
An opportunity for the public to provide input. 
 
The Arborist Report, Biological Technical Report, tree survey, map of the course, and information about the 
Community Meeting and parking map can be found on the City’s website at:
