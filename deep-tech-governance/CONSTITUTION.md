# AXM Deep-Tech Governance Constitution

Release: AXM-DEEPTECH-001 / 1.0.0

1. A human selects every real target. The machine does not discover, rank, or silently substitute a company.
2. Public observation remains public observation until company-controlled evidence and attributable authority admit a stronger state.
3. Relationship, instrument, consideration, capital, capability, claim, evidence, rights, authority, decision, and exception states remain independent.
4. Signed does not imply effective, funded, performed, integrated, accepted, or deployed.
5. Partner capability does not transfer to the target without target-specific integration evidence.
6. Simulation, synthetic media, generated geometry, and model output remain distinguishable from observed physical state.
7. A single demonstration does not establish repeatability, production readiness, customer acceptance, or operational deployment.
8. Public title, affiliation, attendance, introduction, selection, silence, or software UI state creates no company authority.
9. Every material claim carries source references, allowed language, prohibited upgrades, review conditions, and a correction path.
10. Every technical promotion binds the exact physical configuration, software/model revision, sensor calibration, environment, procedure, telemetry, acceptance criteria, result, limitations, and acceptance authority.
11. Every material right is explicit. Background IP, foreground IP, data, publicity, exclusivity, geography, manufacturing, license, termination, and evidence-use rights do not arise from proximity or contribution alone.
12. Bounded search misses establish coverage, not universal absence.
13. Semantic source structure controls when flattened extraction could change chronology, labels, tables, or field relationships.
14. Corrections and successor states preserve the state they supersede.
15. Private source bytes remain outside public releases.
16. External effects remain absent unless a separately authorized adapter is qualified for a declared action.
17. Projections may omit governed material for an audience; they may not change the underlying state.
18. Failure, unknown state, unsupported promotion, and missing authority remain visible.
19. Portable custody and deterministic qualification remain possible without a vendor account.
20. Payment, customer interest, prestige, or partner repetition cannot promote truth, rights, maturity, or authority.
