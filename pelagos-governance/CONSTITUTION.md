# Pelagos Governance Layer Constitution

Release: DDV-PEL-003 / 0.3.0  
Status: operator-qualified public-safe candidate; Pelagos admission required

This constitution governs the public tool and every local workspace created from it.

## Immutable laws

1. **The committed public cartridge is frozen.** A correction creates a new append-only amendment or a later release. It never silently edits the historical baseline.
2. **Public evidence establishes what was published or observed inside its stated scope.** It does not automatically establish performance, legal effectiveness, funding, ownership, endorsement, or authority.
3. **Maturity and authority are independent.** A mature capability may remain unauthorized. A powerful actor cannot promote an immature claim by repeating it.
4. **Relationship, capital, instrument, capability, claim, evidence, rights, and authority states do not substitute for one another.** Signed is not funded. Agreed is not performed. Partner capability is not Pelagos capability. Simulation is not field demonstration.
5. **No retrospective goalpost movement.** A later qualification plan may create a successor state. It cannot downgrade or upgrade an earlier bounded receipt.
6. **Unknown remains visible.** Missing private instruments, identities, terms, rights, and evidence are never filled with plausible narrative.
7. **No silent authority.** Public title, affiliation, introduction, attendance, selection, or silence creates no duty, permission, speaking authority, or power to bind Pelagos.
8. **No silent consideration.** Money, factory access, engineering, introductions, data, equipment, publicity, geography, exclusivity, maintenance, and exit rights remain explicit and separately reviewable.
9. **Private source bytes do not enter the public release.** Local intake hashes files in the browser. Bytes are not retained by the runtime.
10. **Every material claim has an owner, evidence boundary, allowed language, prohibited upgrade, audience, review trigger, and correction path.** A disclaimer elsewhere on the site does not cure an unbounded claim.
11. **Every material decision retains actor, authority source, evidence, rationale, prior state, new state, and receipt lineage.** The interface can record a decision; it cannot create the authority behind it.
12. **Local object changes create successors, not overwrites.** The effective view may project the latest state, but the earlier object, its receipt, and the supersession edge remain recoverable.
13. **A working copy requires explicit admission.** Pelagos must name the decision, technical, communications, and custody owners and cite the exact authority source. Public deployment alone does not convert the candidate into company state.
14. **Single-writer custody is the default.** One designated custodian operates the authoritative local copy until a separately qualified synchronization mechanism exists. Parallel browser copies are independent branches, not silent collaboration.
15. **External effects remain absent.** The tool cannot send email, schedule, pay, publish, sign, accept, represent, procure, or write back to a source system.
16. **Public, diligence, counsel, and internal apertures are projections over one state.** A projection may omit material; it may not change the underlying truth state.
17. **No hidden aggregate score.** Fatal gaps cannot be canceled by strengths elsewhere. Stress results remain per scenario and per gate.
18. **Portable custody is mandatory.** The operator can export the complete local state, verify its ledger, encrypt a backup, restore it, and hand it to a cold successor without a vendor account.
19. **Failure must be visible.** Storage loss, parser failure, unsupported formats, duplicate sources, broken receipt chains, missing authority, stale claims, unresolved legal residue, and workspace divergence are surfaced rather than normalized away.
20. **Payment cannot promote truth or authority.** Commercial engagement may authorize work. It cannot make a claim true, settle a right, prove performance, or grant corporate power absent the corresponding evidence and instrument.

## Public boundary

The release is an outside-in reconstruction from public Pelagos sources. It is not a Pelagos system of record until admitted, an accepted legal conclusion, legal advice, an export-control determination, an investment recommendation, technical certification, customer endorsement, procurement status, or proof that private instruments exist in the form inferred by public announcements.

## Change control

A change to an implementation may replace code while preserving these laws. A change to an immutable law requires an explicit owner decision recorded in repository history. A correction to the public baseline requires a successor cartridge or release receipt naming the authority, evidence, changed proposition, and superseded state.
