# Resolution law

## 1. Object

Every surface must classify the thing it operates on. A page, file, case, place, protocol, organ, claim set, legislation item, or identity release must have a stable identifier, defined boundaries, lifecycle, and explicit exclusions.

A feature list is not an object model. A section title is not a boundary.

## 2. Actors, roles, and authority

The record must name the functional actors that create, observe, sponsor, validate, decide, execute, benefit, maintain, and inherit the object.

No named person is required to playtest an internal seat. The role is exercised by its information needs, lawful controls, refusal conditions, acceptance criteria, and handoff obligations.

A role selector that changes only copy or filtering fails this gate.

## 3. Source custody

Every material claim must resolve to one of four states:

- source-bound;
- derived from named source-bound records;
- asserted with an identified actor and force;
- unresolved.

Live and imported sources require retrieval time, source time when available, exact request or intake identity, content digest, parser or transformation version, attribution, rights, cache or retention rule, claim scope, and failure state.

Generated media cannot substitute for available source evidence. It may illustrate a declared reference world, fixture, or hypothesis only when its status is visible.

## 4. Mechanism fidelity

The implementation must do the thing the product says it does.

Seven scale buttons must reveal seven different operating apertures. Five seats must create five different lawful projections. An overlay must remain registered to the geometry it describes. A handoff must reconstruct the record in a separate successor context.

Counts, labels, and route existence do not establish mechanism fidelity.

## 5. Live data and failure states

A live claim requires evidence from the actual provider or first-party capture path. Missing credentials, empty coverage, access denial, timeout, stale cache, parser drift, and partial source loss are distinct states and must remain distinct in the interface and receipts.

A fallback is legitimate only when it narrows the claim. A fallback must never silently replace a live or observed object with fabricated data.

## 6. Interaction

Every primary control must produce a material, inspectable state change. The audit must retain the before state, action, after state, affected canonical objects, authority effect, and export consequence.

The complete workflow must be played from every functional seat. The page must also survive keyboard-only use, touch, repeated activation, back and forward navigation, refresh, interrupted work, and restoration.

## 7. Visual and typographic constitution

Every public surface needs a written visual constitution that governs:

- type sources and provenance;
- display measure, line breaks, kerning or tracking ranges, and optical fit;
- running-text measure and hierarchy;
- semantic colors and monochrome survival;
- section families and transition logic;
- image and diagram fidelity;
- annotation density;
- light and dark parity;
- focus and motion;
- compact-width composition.

Giant type is not automatically authored type. A detailed hero followed by generic blocks is a site-wide failure.

Visual qualification requires retained goldens for representative desktop, laptop, tablet, mobile, and compact widths. It also requires measured overflow, clipping, contrast, font loading, image decode, and interaction-state captures.

## 8. Responsive and accessible operation

The information hierarchy must survive every supported viewport. Hiding navigation, clipping text, suppressing overflow, shrinking touch targets, or moving content behind a sticky header fails the gate even when the document reports no horizontal scroll.

Keyboard order, focus visibility, labels, landmarks, reduced motion, screen-reader naming, zoom, contrast, and touch targets are release evidence, not cleanup work.

## 9. Negative, stress, and offline evidence

Every surface must exercise the failures that could corrupt its claim:

- malformed, duplicated, contradictory, oversized, or unsupported inputs;
- missing, stale, blocked, or rate-limited sources;
- local storage or IndexedDB loss;
- network loss and low bandwidth;
- provider or supplier exit;
- interrupted export or import;
- unauthorized external-effect requests;
- compact-width and dense-content stress;
- cold successor with no conversational context.

The expected result is often refusal or a narrowed claim. A graceful refusal is a successful test.

## 10. Continuity and succession

The canonical record must be exportable, inspectable, and reconstructable without the original session, browser profile, maintainer, vendor, or conversational memory.

The successor package must state what is authoritative, what is derived, what remains open, what can rot, how to verify integrity, and which outside effects are still held.

## 11. Provenance, rights, and privacy

Every retained or rendered asset needs source, author or provider when available, rights or license, transformation history, privacy class, storage rule, and allowed use.

Private source bytes, personal addresses, participant identifiers, and protected communications must not enter a public release by convenience. Redaction must be explicit and reversible only inside the authorized boundary.

## 12. Qualification package

A release qualification must contain more than booleans. It must preserve:

- exact artifact identities and hashes;
- source and transformation receipts;
- browser and viewport campaign results;
- interaction traces;
- visual goldens and measured typography;
- negative and outage results;
- role and authority replay;
- export and cold-successor replay;
- known limitations and open defects.

A legacy qualification remains valid for its historical test scope. It cannot be broadened after the fact.

## Release rule

A surface may be marked `qualified` only when every mandatory gate is `pass`, every evidence path exists, the retained receipts identify the tested artifact exactly, and no critical finding remains open.

Any other honest state is acceptable. A false PASS is not.
