Protocol boundary

Fail-closed rule. A hidden strategic holdout, negative adverse margin, failed N-1 capacity, restoration below threshold, or certified saturation forces a HOLD. The demonstrator never converts a technical finding directly into military force.

This synthetic exercise separates evidence collection, managed access, technical findings, services and rollback, consequences and assurance, and independent review. A role may not perform another role’s action. The facilitator can advance, hold, reinforce, and recertify, but cannot rewrite a technical finding.
