# Breakout Parity Demonstrative Verification Exercise Protocol

Protocol ID: `BP-DV/1.0`

## Purpose

The exercise tests an institutional chain, rather than a sensor or treaty text in isolation. It begins after a claimed entry baseline and forces covered threats, decoys, ambiguous evidence, a critical-node loss, and restoration obligations through role-separated queues.

## Exercise questions

1. Does the baseline challenge expose an uncovered strategic lineage before deeper reductions proceed?
2. Does the system preserve persistent identity across inspection, rollback, recurrence, transfer, and restoration?
3. Can the evidence and inspection cells improve confidence without imposing consequences?
4. Can the findings panel issue a graded finding while preserving uncertainty and dissent?
5. Can the response body act quickly without manufacturing or rewriting the finding?
6. Can a corrected actor receive promised restoration on schedule?
7. Can the system survive loss of the largest relevant node using prequalified reserve?
8. Does the gate hold before the lower-tail margin, restoration reliability, coverage, or portfolio capacity becomes unsafe?

## Seven seats

1. Facilitator and Gate Authority
2. Evidence Cell
3. Inspection and Managed Access Team
4. Technical Findings Panel
5. Services and Rollback Secretariat
6. Assurance and Response Council
7. Review Chamber and Inspector General

One person may cold-walk all seats. A serious exercise should assign separate participants and prevent private facilitator truth from entering role decisions.

## Ninety-minute format

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 0-10 min | Read scope, evidence boundary, role authorities, and gate criteria |
| 10-20 min | Challenge the entry baseline and establish the initial registry |
| 20-40 min | Process the first signals and decoys through evidence and access queues |
| 40-55 min | Inject N-1 failure, contradictory evidence, and public pressure |
| 55-70 min | Issue findings, response measures, rollback terms, and dissent |
| 70-80 min | Verify cure and execute or miss restoration |
| 80-85 min | Run adverse-bound recertification |
| 85-90 min | Record gate decision, failures, corrections, and assigned owners |

## Stronger experimental gate

```text
adverse-bound p10 portfolio margin >= 6 months
probability of any breach <= 5 percent
restoration on time >= 95 percent
N-1 capacity passed
no known uncovered strategic holdout
portfolio load inside the certified saturation boundary
```

The values are research thresholds. They are not measurements of existing institutions.

## Required exercise receipts

- exact scenario and protocol versions;
- participant and role list;
- threat-registry state transitions;
- evidence provenance and confidence changes;
- rejected authority-overreach attempts;
- findings, dissent, and appeal;
- action activation and effect times;
- rollback, cure, and restoration times;
- resource failure and reserve activation;
- final gate metrics and disposition;
- unresolved interfaces and assigned test owners.

## Fail-closed doctrine

A failed gate stops advancement to the next reduction state. It preserves monitoring, custody, crisis controls, assurance, rollback, and restoration. It does not automatically authorize preventive force or concealment of the failure.
