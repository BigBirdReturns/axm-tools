# Institutional Mapping and Precedent Boundary

The V11 roles are functional abstractions. They are informed by existing public practice but do not assign new authority to the institutions named below.

## Evidence and national analysis

The CTBTO's 2026 regional National Data Centre exercise is designed to practice the workflow from initial event alert through national reporting, using International Monitoring System data, International Data Centre products, national data, and fusion methods. This supports a separate evidence-analysis seat and a handoff from international technical products to national interpretation.

The IAEA has described multidisciplinary State Evaluation Groups as developing state-level approaches, updating evaluations with new information, producing annual summaries, and recommending safeguards conclusions. This supports a persistent evaluation record rather than isolated facility events.

## Managed access and role-separated inspection

The IPNDV NuDiVe exercise assigned participants to inspector and inspected-state roles to simulate verified dismantlement and test procedures and interactions. Its Belgian Measurement Campaign separately tested candidate measurement techniques. These precedents support role-separated exercise design and protected information boundaries.

UNIDIR's Menzingen experiment tested a model inspection protocol for verifying absence at a storage site, including procedures, radiation measurements using active sources, satellite imagery, and pre- and post-inspection workshops.

## Demonstrative verification before a treaty

UNIDIR's *Verification Without a Treaty* proposes detailed protocols, open data rather than intelligence estimates, and potentially independent observers for states seeking to demonstrate compliance outside a completed treaty regime. This supports a public synthetic demonstrator and a formal receipt structure while leaving sovereign legal authority unchanged.

## Notification exchange

The Hague Code of Conduct uses voluntary pre-launch notifications and annual declarations coordinated through Austria as Immediate Central Contact. The ICC coordinates exchange. It is not represented here as an enforcement body.

## Deliberate non-mapping

No current public institution is presumed to possess the complete Breakout Parity mandate. The Services and Rollback Secretariat, Assurance and Response Council, Review Chamber, portfolio scheduler, and cross-domain gate are proposed interfaces. Their legality, sponsorship, and institutional home remain subjects for external review.
