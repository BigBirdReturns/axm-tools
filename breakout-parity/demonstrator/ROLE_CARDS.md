# Role Cards

## Facilitator and Gate Authority

**Receives:** gate metrics, resource status, known coverage defects, and role receipts.

**May:** advance the scenario, activate prequalified flex, reinforce qualified capacity, declare a hold, and recertify.

**May not:** alter evidence, compel a technical finding, erase dissent, or convert a finding directly into force.

**Control question:** Is the portfolio still inside the adverse-bound operating envelope after the largest critical-node loss?

## Evidence Cell

**Receives:** declarations, open records, commercial records, synthetic sensor products, national summaries, and prior lineage history.

**May:** triage, fuse, qualify provenance, identify gaps, challenge the baseline, and request clarification or access.

**May not:** impose consequences or declare compliance.

**Control question:** What does the evidence support, what remains ambiguous, and what collection would distinguish competing explanations?

## Inspection and Managed Access Team

**Receives:** an agreed access objective, protected-area constraints, chain-of-custody rules, and a sampling plan.

**May:** execute managed access, synthetic sampling, tags and seals, and cure verification.

**May not:** exceed the access mandate, disclose unrelated protected information, or issue the final finding.

**Control question:** Did the inspection answer the technical objective without converting access into unrestricted exposure?

## Technical Findings Panel

**Receives:** provenance-bearing evidence packets, access reports, laboratory results, limitations, and competing interpretations.

**May:** issue no-concern, unresolved-anomaly, significant-anomaly, or verified-violation findings and preserve dissent.

**May not:** impose sanctions, suspend services, authorize force, or rewrite raw evidence.

**Control question:** Which finding is justified by the record, at what confidence, and with which unresolved alternatives?

## Services and Rollback Secretariat

**Receives:** the technical finding, declared corrective actions, service entitlements, security commitments, and restoration rules.

**May:** offer bounded disclosure, access, material transfer, facility conversion, monitoring, cure, and objective restoration.

**May not:** suppress an adverse finding or use nuclear correction to adjudicate unrelated disputes.

**Control question:** Is truthful correction materially safer than concealment, acceleration, or recurrence?

## Assurance and Response Council

**Receives:** a technical finding, response ladder, legal authority, exposed-party needs, and restoration constraints.

**May:** apply reversible service, procurement, or financial measures and defensive reinforcement within its mandate.

**May not:** manufacture the finding, punish unrelated conduct through the nuclear channel, or treat force as an automatic technical output.

**Control question:** Can the response deny coercive advantage before the clock expires while preserving a route back to compliance?

## Review Chamber and Inspector General

**Receives:** provenance, role actions, findings, dissent, consequence activation, appeal, and restoration receipts.

**May:** audit process, identify role overreach, correct false action, preserve minority findings, and report unmet restoration.

**May not:** invent technical facts or substitute itself for the authorized consequence body.

**Control question:** Did every actor remain inside authority, and did the system correct error as reliably as it escalated concern?
