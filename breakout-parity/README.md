# Breakout Parity

**A testable control architecture for post-deterrence stability**

Author: Jonathan Sandhu  
Public concept-prior date: 2026-07-26  
Framework version: 4.0.0-draft-release  
Project codename: Omega Accord Lab

Breakout Parity treats nuclear proliferation as a control-system problem. Destroying an inventory or facility does not end proliferation when technical knowledge, motive, restart capacity, weapon-usable material, and political grievance remain. The framework therefore asks whether detection, technical attribution, and effective response can complete before a violator converts breakout into coercive advantage, while compliant states receive a superior package of security, energy reliability, economic access, status, and institutional continuity.

The governing control criterion is:

```text
T_detect + T_attribute + T_respond
<
T_breakout_to_coercive_advantage
```

The architecture contains four service-producing operating systems followed by a sovereign constitutional bargain:

1. Decision-time protection, crisis communication, notification, command-system restraint, and material security.
2. A compliance dividend built from assured fuel services, finance, scientific access, conventional protection, and protected rollback.
3. Federated verification, evidence-provenance rules, graded findings, reversible consequences, and automatic restoration after cure.
4. Conversion finance and successor missions for laboratories, production sites, bases, contractors, workers, and host communities.
5. Aggregate military declarations, no-growth ceilings, fissile-production restraint, verified dismantlement, monitored material custody, managed latency, and withdrawal-surviving obligations.

The adoption unit is a bounded claim or pilot with a named owner, antecedents, failure condition, receipt, and correction path. Publication does not imply endorsement by any institution named in the framework.

## Evidence boundary

The simulation results are assumption-driven mechanism experiments, not empirical forecasts. Their use is comparative: to expose dependencies, sequencing failures, and conditions under which a regime becomes brittle. The concept-prior capsule can be evaluated without accepting any modeled percentage.

## Control question

Can any actor convert retained knowledge, weapon-usable material, industrial capacity, or alliance panic into unilateral coercive advantage before detection, attribution, and effective collective response remove that advantage, while compliance supplies the superior package of survival, energy, status, and domestic continuity?
