Literature status correction

The public date establishes this exact formulation, not priority over its component ideas. The original capsule followed a targeted institutional-capability review, systems synthesis, and simulation. It did not follow a field-wide literature review. Strong prior art exists for reconstitution, managed latency, assurance, verification, organizational failure, conversion, compliance, and most proliferation motives.

The remaining candidate contribution is narrower and under test: integration around time to coercive advantage, resource-constrained portfolio response, executable restoration, gate-controlled sequencing, and a functional retirement test for mutual assured destruction. Six external-review drafts remain unsent while the genealogy and model repairs are incorporated.

The governing conditions

T_detect + T_attribute + T_respond < T_breakout_to_coercive_advantage Expected value of compliance > Expected value of concealed breakout or continued possession

The first condition denies a useful first-mover interval. The second removes the motive to defect. The literature amendment requires distributions over several stages of coercive advantage, scarce response capacity, and explicit escalation effects rather than a single deterministic clock.

Four operating systems and one constitutional bargain

1. Decision and notification

Protect decision time, crisis channels, launch and exercise notification, authorization integrity, command-system restraint, and material security.

2. Compliance dividend and rollback

Make compliance materially superior through energy services, finance, conventional protection, scientific access, and a survivable route to disclose and cure violations.

3. Verification, response, restoration

Separate evidence-based findings from political consequences, begin with reversible measures, and specify objective restoration after cure.

4. Conversion and continuity

Commit successor missions and transition finance before reductions concentrate losses in the institutions capable of rebuilding the arsenal.

5. Constitutional bargain

Admit military inventories and production estates into declarations, no-growth limits, verification, dismantlement, monitored custody, managed latency, and withdrawal-surviving obligations.

OA-003Compliance dividend

Established policy family; bundled-service synthesis

OA-004Protected rollback

Case-grounded design synthesis

Credible security and restoration promises remain difficult to bind across political turnover.

OA-005Findings-consequences separation

Established principle; interface specification

The candidate delta is the provenance, dissent, appeal, and authority handoff.

OA-006Automatic restoration

Potentially distinctive; weakly mapped

Legal feasibility, fragmented authority, and strategic cure gaming remain open.

OA-007Conversion before reductions

Strong prior art; sequencing hypothesis

Conversion can create support while preserving dangerous latent capacity.

OA-008Strategically complete coverage

Regional synthesis; threshold unvalidated

Partial regimes can still reduce risk, and reset capacity must be computed regionally.

OA-009Managed latency

Mature field; end-state integration hypothesis

Latency itself can deter, coerce, trigger prevention, and generate rearmament races.

OA-010MAD retirement test

Functional integration hypothesis

Security, identity, status, bureaucracy, bargaining, and order functions require separate treatment.

OA-011One-thousand-day qualification

Programme integration proposal

Exercise success does not establish operational reliability or political adoption.

OA-012Four systems plus a bargain

Architecture synthesis; novelty unestablished

Every institutional seam must have an owner, timeout, appeal, and failure state.

Evidence boundary

This page establishes public existence of the original formulation and the subsequent literature correction. It does not establish that the complete binary estate has been mirrored here, or that any institution received, acknowledged, reviewed, tested, sponsored, or adopted the framework.

The current review is a structured first-pass seed corpus rather than a saturated review. Forty-one entries remain bibliographic seeds pending lawful full-text acquisition and coding. Quantitative simulations remain assumption-driven mechanism experiments and may not be represented as empirical forecasts.
