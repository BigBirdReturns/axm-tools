Breakout Parity

A testable control architecture for post-deterrence stability. The framework controls material, reconstitution pathways, incentives, response time, alliance confidence, and domestic continuity because technical knowledge cannot be destroyed.

The governing conditions

T_detect + T_attribute + T_respond < T_breakout_to_coercive_advantage Expected value of compliance > Expected value of concealed breakout or continued possession

The first condition denies a useful first-mover interval. The second removes the motive to defect. A regime that satisfies only one remains unstable.

Four operating systems and one constitutional bargain

1. Decision and notification

Protect decision time, crisis channels, launch and exercise notification, authorization integrity, command-system restraint, and material security.

2. Compliance dividend and rollback

Make compliance materially superior through energy services, finance, conventional protection, scientific access, and a survivable route to disclose and cure violations.

3. Verification, response, restoration

Separate evidence-based findings from political consequences, begin with reversible measures, and specify objective restoration after cure.

4. Conversion and continuity

Commit successor missions and transition finance before reductions concentrate losses in the institutions capable of rebuilding the arsenal.

5. Constitutional bargain

Admit military inventories and production estates into declarations, no-growth limits, verification, dismantlement, monitored custody, managed latency, and withdrawal-surviving obligations.

Twelve falsifiable claims

The full mechanism, antecedent map, responsible actor classes, first action, and falsification test for every claim are preserved in the claim ledger.

OA-001Interdiction recurrence

Derived synthesis

Inventory destruction does not end proliferation while knowledge, motive, restart capacity, and grievance survive.

OA-002Breakout-parity inequality

Control criterion

Detection, attribution, and response must complete before breakout becomes coercive advantage.

OA-003Compliance dividend

Derived synthesis

Compliance must supply a superior package of security, energy, finance, status, science, and continuity.

OA-004Protected rollback

Institutional design

Disclosure and cure cannot predictably become a pathway to regime removal.

OA-005Findings-consequences separation

Governance synthesis

Evidence-based technical findings must remain institutionally separate from coercive political decisions.

OA-006Automatic restoration

Control rail

Every reversible penalty requires objective cure conditions and a scheduled step-down.

OA-007Conversion before reductions

Political-economy criterion

Successor missions and transition finance must precede concentrated arsenal-estate losses.

OA-008Strategically complete coverage

Gate criterion

Deep reductions wait until every actor capable of resetting the security equilibrium is covered.

OA-009Managed latency

End state

Reconstitution pathways remain visible, bounded, symmetric, slow, and strategically unusable.

OA-010MAD retirement test

Acceptance test

MAD retires only when nuclear forces no longer supply a unique service that replacements cannot deliver.

OA-011One-thousand-day qualification

Programme design

Replacement systems qualify under stress before deep dismantlement becomes a programme objective.

OA-012Four systems plus a bargain

Framework architecture

No-regrets systems mature under available authority before sovereign military symmetry is negotiated.

Evidence boundary

This page establishes public existence of the stated concepts and their test conditions. It does not establish that the complete binary estate has been mirrored here, or that any institution received, acknowledged, reviewed, tested, sponsored, or adopted the framework. Those states require separate receipts.

The simulation is an assumption-driven mechanism experiment. It supports comparative reasoning about dependencies and failure modes, not empirical prediction.

Control question

Can any actor convert retained knowledge, weapon-usable material, industrial capacity, or alliance panic into unilateral coercive advantage before detection, attribution, and effective collective response remove that advantage, while compliance supplies the superior package of survival, energy, status, and domestic continuity?
