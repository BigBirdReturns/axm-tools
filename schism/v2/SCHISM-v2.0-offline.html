03 / From the June proposition to executable evidence

Continuity becomes a claim
you can test.

Jonathan Sandhu / SCHISM 2.0

The obligation survives the supplier.

The June 26 SCHISM dossier treated software control as a lifecycle question: what can the customer retain, verify, change and recover when a supplier relationship changes? Its substantive demand was an observable continuity test. The retained original and edition history establish the origin of that proposition; the application above supplies a bounded software case.

The record carries the work.

November's Industrial Continuity implementation separates source revisions, calculation results and declared reviews. A correction invalidates the standing of dependent work before a new calculation occurs. The handoff carries the earlier evidence as well as its successor, allowing a receiver to reconstruct how the conclusion changed. This test preserves that existing contract and exposes the origin's closure as an explicit state transition.

The independent receiver adds a different implementation language to the evidence. Its qualification must show that the delivered program reconstructs the same bounded calculations and refuses the declared failure cases on the reported hosts. The recorded executions above determine how much of that claim this edition has earned. A browser replay, a source-code listing and a native execution receipt establish different facts.

Acceptance includes the inconvenient result.

The related Cantos maintainer evidence preserves an incomparable outcome when its recorded model runs miss their acceptance floor; recipient replay and changed pricing leave that gate intact. Decision Floor's peer exchange preserves disagreement between separately held records. The same evidentiary discipline applies here: missing dependencies, stale decisions and failed imports remain visible outcomes.

A specific software result has a specific boundary.

A successful run establishes continuity for this synthetic, two-product planning recipe under the tested implementations and conditions. Raw inputs remain declared or operator-supplied records; a review label establishes no authenticated identity or operational permission. Consistent hashes and replay can expose a changed or internally false conclusion while a self-consistent fabricated source history remains fabricated. Hardware portability, secure boot, physical recovery, radio behavior and any supplier's deployed system remain outside this software test.

The useful result is a retained record and a receiver that can either continue the work or state exactly why it cannot accept it.
