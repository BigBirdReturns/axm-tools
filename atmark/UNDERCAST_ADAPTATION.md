# UnderCast's source/display method, adapted to merchandise

This is an adaptation of the existing UnderCast photo and evidence method, not
a claim that UnderCast's performer-specific admission machinery has certified
computer inventory. The source project was read without changes.

Method authorities: `BigBirdReturns/undercast`, `MEDIA.md` and
`docs/MEDIA-AUDIT.md`, as read on 28 October 2026. They distinguish exact-subject
identity from presentation, retained source bytes from derived displays, and
review from publication. Source URLs and content hashes establish provenance;
they do not establish subject correctness. The new collector is bounded and
source-specific rather than a claim that the MediaWiki adapter supports eBay.

The mapping is:

- A performer/role identity becomes an exact listing ID plus a separately
  unresolved physical stock identity. Model-family similarity never joins units.
- A sourced image becomes a source-URL / hash / timestamp record. Shared URLs
  trigger review, not automatic reuse across different model names.
- Source selection and display preparation remain separate. A 3650 listing
  carrying a 3630-labelled image enters a hold; a new framing cannot fix identity.
- Original bytes remain untouched. Full-frame CSS presentation is distinct from
  the original, and a local crop/rotation export has a retained recipe and new hash.
- An observer extraction is labelled as such, not represented as raw eBay API
  custody. Public-web snapshot completeness remains unresolved.
- Accepted parsing is separate from permission to advertise or sell. All output
  is review-only; actual stock, rights, condition and commercial authority belong
  to the seller. The photo studio records local operator assertions without
  inventing independent or authenticated human consensus.
- A missing or wrong image stays missing or held. No generative replacement
  becomes evidence of used merchandise or its condition.

The real run's terminal product is a public, independent preview plus portable
code and review data. Whole-store reconciliation, fresh physical photographs,
authenticated seller writeback and measured commercial uplift are outside this
completed preview. The kit names those boundaries rather than manufacturing
success receipts for work it did not perform.
