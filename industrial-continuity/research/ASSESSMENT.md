## Opportunity selection

The candidate comparison uses ordinal analyst judgments, not a measured market score or ROI. Repair/spares handoff has strong existing AXM donor reuse but can remain asset-centric. Generic conversational SOP assistance overlaps heavily with OpsMate's stated offering. End-to-end live automation has higher integration and authority requirements than the available evidence supports. Cross-shift process/economic decision continuity has the strongest combined fit: it uses ThroughPut's operational objective, OpsMate's review/handoff sequence and Bullotta's asset/process distinction, while retaining a falsifiable software boundary.

The selected build joins six source families: ERP demand and contribution; MES routing; available process capacity; CMMS parts/recovery economics; QMS release; and a versioned review procedure. Each comes from a declared synthetic record. Sources remain separate and versioned; the workbench needs only their decision-relevant fragments. No public pump reading is presented as evidence for these fabricated process quantities.

## What this build must demonstrate

A feasible intervention initially improves planned contribution after cost. A customer-economics correction makes that intervention unnecessary. The source change must first invalidate current standing without overwriting history. A new evaluation must reconstruct the changed recommendation. A prior review must not silently move to the successor. A recipient must independently reproduce both decisions from the export in a clean offline client, including their source frontiers. A changed saved conclusion must fail even when its hashes are recomputed. Missing required inputs must remain unknown.

The direct commercial question is whether this portable decision record lowers reconstruction, integration or handoff cost in a real workflow. A competitor's equivalent complete export at lower adoption cost defeats or narrows the differentiation. Product adoption, implementation cost, receiving-team acceptance, real authority, source authenticity and actual operating value remain unmeasured. The next production transition belongs to customer-owned mappings, identities, permissions and outcome measurements; this prototype grants none of them.
