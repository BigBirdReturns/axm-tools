# Useful Plant v30 operator gate

The candidate does not contain an acceptance control. Visual admission is a separate operator act governed by `OPERATOR_REVIEW_CONTRACT.json` and recorded outside the candidate source tree with `operator_decision.py`.

The committed `OPERATOR_DECISION_TEMPLATE.json` must remain `PENDING`, with operator visual acceptance `ABSENT`, no operator, no rationale, no bound review receipts, and every criterion pending. `test_operator_decision.py` adversarially checks that a candidate cannot preaccept one criterion, raise release authority, accept with a failed criterion, request revision without identifying a failure, or survive receipt drift.

A future `ACCEPTED` visual decision may establish this surface as a visual parent for the next bounded assembly step. It cannot authorize merge, release, public-route assembly, substitution of the missing exact v29 archive or Plant media, insurance action, property scoring, resident ranking, punitive enforcement, or any other external effect.

The seven criteria are the living object's visual primacy, explainable and rejectable placement, visible evidence burden, a completed use-and-return loop, materially different seat authority, legible household stop authority, and an assistance-first firewall.

Control question: after reviewing the retained screenshots and operating the candidate, does the surface satisfy all seven criteria without relaxing any exact-parent, release, merge, route, or external-effect hold?
