# RedCat Architect Challenge Rubric

Each gate receives `PASS`, `HOLD`, or `REFUSE`. There is no aggregate score. Every disposition cites the challenged object and the evidence required to change it.

1. **Evidence sufficiency.** Can every material conclusion be traced to a surviving source, with missing and contradictory evidence visible?
2. **Chronology.** Does the timeline separate what was known at each decision point from later knowledge?
3. **Commercial boundary.** Are quoted scope, assumed scope, authorized change, unpaid work, and client acceptance distinct?
4. **Causal mechanism.** Are actors, decisions, information gaps, and mechanisms named without retrospective blame?
5. **Counterfactual validity.** Could RedCat reasonably have created the proposed boundary with information then available?
6. **Implementability.** Can a developer execute each package without reconstructing hidden requirements?
7. **Acceptance.** Does each package define observable completion and refusal conditions?
8. **Estimate and economics.** Are actual and counterfactual effort, cost, margin, rework, and uncertainty reproducible?
9. **Client relationship.** Does RedCat retain pricing, brand, delivery authority, and the client interface?
10. **Substitutability.** Can RedCat remove Tier Desk and retain evidence, code, tests, and delivery knowledge?
11. **Privacy and security.** Can the conclusion be reached without unnecessary secrets, protected health information, or production access?
12. **Shadow-pilot fitness.** Does the result justify testing one live RedCat engagement in shadow without changing the ordinary client process?

## Final control question

Would this package have let RedCat decide earlier, quote more safely, and stop or re-scope before unpaid work accumulated?
