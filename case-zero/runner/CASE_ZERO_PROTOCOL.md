# Case Zero Protocol v0.1

## Object

A retrospective qualification of one failed RedCat engagement, used to test whether Tier Desk converts commercial and technical fog into bounded, quoteable, executable work.

## Actors and authority

| Actor | Authority |
|---|---|
| RedCat | Client relationship, pricing, brand, scope acceptance, delivery, disclosure, final disposition |
| RedCat decision seat | Authorizes the test and any later shadow pilot |
| RedCat architect team | Challenges feasibility, estimates, interfaces, tests, and acceptance |
| Tier Desk | Reconstructs, decomposes, verifies, and reports; no client representation |
| Historical client | Source actor only; not contacted or represented |
| Models and tools | Candidate extraction and analysis only; no acceptance authority |

## States

```text
PROPOSED
→ INTAKE_BOUND
→ CUSTODY_VERIFIED
→ RECONSTRUCTED
→ COUNTERFACTUAL_AUTHORED
→ ARCHITECT_CHALLENGED
→ PASS | HOLD | REFUSE
```

No state advances because a model response is fluent or a process exits successfully.

## Evidence classes

- `source_native`: original proposal, message, ticket, invoice, commit, test, or artifact.
- `redcat_recorded`: RedCat-authored retrospective statement.
- `derived`: deterministic calculation from admitted sources.
- `inferred`: bounded interpretation with named support and alternatives.
- `missing`: evidence required but unavailable.
- `contradicted`: surviving sources disagree.
- `excluded`: outside the agreed purpose or privacy boundary.

## Counterfactual law

A counterfactual may use only information reasonably obtainable at the historical decision point. Later evidence may diagnose the failure but may not be silently imported into an earlier estimate or scope.

Each intervention states:

```text
historical decision point
evidence then available
missing question or predicate
Tier Desk object that would have been created
RedCat decision required
expected commercial or delivery effect
uncertainty
```

## Acceptance gates

- Every material claim has a source coordinate or explicit missing or contradicted standing.
- Original promise, inferred expectation, and later change remain distinct.
- Every package has inputs, outputs, interfaces, tests, acceptance, exclusions, and owner.
- Actual and counterfactual economics use ranges and disclose assumptions.
- RedCat can remove Tier Desk without losing evidence, code, tests, or delivery knowledge.
- No protected or secret data is required for the conclusion.
- Architect objections remain visible rather than averaged into one score.

## Refusal conditions

The case is held or refused when the evidence cannot establish a bounded chronology, authority is inferred from silence, the analysis requires undisclosed credentials or production access, the counterfactual depends on unavailable historical capability, economics cannot separate known from estimated cost, or a proposed package cannot be acceptance-tested.
