# Tier Desk × RedCat: Case Zero

## The test

RedCat nominates one real engagement that went wrong and was never paid. The surviving record should remain commercially and technically ugly. Tier Desk must reconstruct the case without inventing missing messages, hours, promises, acceptance conditions, or client decisions.

## Ownership

RedCat retains the client relationship, pricing, commercial terms, brand, scope authority, delivery, disclosure, and every client-facing decision. Tier Desk operates behind RedCat as qualification infrastructure. The historical client is not contacted or represented.

## Inputs

Useful evidence includes proposals, statements of work, call notes, messages, tickets, estimates, invoices, architecture notes, repository metadata, tests, scope changes, and final disposition. RedCat may keep the raw corpus local and release only manifests or selected redacted evidence.

Credentials, production secrets, protected health information, and unnecessary personal data are excluded.

## Outputs

Tier Desk returns:

1. an evidence-addressed engagement timeline;
2. a promise and scope ledger;
3. a scope-change ledger;
4. an acceptance ledger;
5. an actual work and economics ledger;
6. a causal failure mechanism;
7. the bounded PoC that should have existed;
8. developer-ready implementation packages;
9. actual-versus-counterfactual economics; and
10. a shadow-pilot contract.

## Pass condition

RedCat architects can trace every material conclusion, identify missing evidence, implement the proposed packages, and agree that the counterfactual would have reduced unpaid scoping, estimate error, rework, or acceptance churn without moving the customer relationship away from RedCat.

## Failure condition

The test fails if Tier Desk produces a persuasive story without evidence, imports hindsight into earlier decisions, assumes authority RedCat did not possess, hides uncertainty inside one score, or cannot translate the reconstruction into work RedCat could quote and deliver.

## Control question

Would this package have let RedCat decide earlier, quote more safely, and stop or re-scope before unpaid work accumulated?
