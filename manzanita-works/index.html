ESSENTIAL PILOTAGE

Hand-holding is capacity when its authority boundary is explicit

Pilotage helps somebody reach the correct tool, program, professional, workshop, form, service, or decision-maker. It does not impersonate the person who actually owns the service or legal authority.

01Need appears

“I do not know where to start.”

02Pilot translates

Reduce ambiguity, gather the minimum evidence, identify the correct path.

03Provider boundary remains visible

Mechanic, lawyer, social worker, instructor, program steward, or other accountable provider retains the substantive role.

04Recipient acknowledges

The person confirms what assistance actually occurred.

05Steward accepts

Eligible contribution is recognized only under recorded program policy.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Navigation friction is often the real barrier to access.

A tool can exist, a service can exist, and a grant can exist while the person who needs it still cannot traverse the institutional path. Pilotage makes that otherwise invisible labor legible without pretending it is the underlying professional service.

Can do
orient, interpret process, prepare handoff, accompany, teach system use
Cannot do
manufacture authority, accept itself, impersonate a licensed provider, silently expose sensitive records
Can earn credit?
Only if the program admits the activity, the recipient acknowledges it, and an authorized steward accepts it
