Held behind its own authority gates

Constitution before convenience

Five rules keep the useful system from becoming the old hierarchy again.

01

Authority never appears by visibility.

Seeing evidence or preparing a packet does not grant entry, spend, work, publication, representation, eligibility, or release authority.

02

Assistance evidence cannot become adverse standing.

Observations gathered to help may not silently become insurance, enforcement, eligibility, property, resident, or punitive scoring.

03

People keep authorship of lived meaning.

Affected participants retain correction, refusal, narrowing, deferral, acceptance, and appeal where applicable.

04

Vendors are replaceable. The community model is not.

MyTurn, Stripe, fundraising platforms, scheduling tools, and future services may solve jobs. None becomes the canonical institutional record by convenience.

05

Continuity is a function, not a person.

Every offer, decision, source, hold, and handoff must survive the absence of the founder, volunteer, operator, or original builder.

The actual organizational ask

Five decisions turn an N=0 working model into one bounded pilot.

No one has to approve the architecture. A pilot starts only when a specific problem has an owner, operating capacity, authority boundary, and stop condition.

  1. 01

    Choose one problem.

    Not “digital transformation.” One real case class worth learning from.

    Pilot scope
  2. 02

    Name the accountable sponsor.

    The person who owns the public promise, budget envelope, organizational boundary, and stop authority.

    Executive owner
  3. 03

    Assign and fund the continuity operator.

    The bounded human operating function cannot default to a founder, advisor, volunteer, or whoever remembers the context.

    Runtime owner
  4. 04

    Name venue, resources, and field partners.

    What is actually available for the pilot, including lawful access, equipment, skills, money, and escalation paths.

    Execution basis
  5. 05

    Set the effect boundary and stop conditions.

    What may happen, what may never happen, what requires separate consent or review, and what makes the pilot stop.

    Authority + safety

If leadership returns tomorrow

Do not schedule a scoping call. Produce one pilot packet.

Choose the problem, identify the organization-owned seats, state what exists, and preserve every blank as a blank. The packet is local preparation only. Exporting it does not contact anyone, accept anything, schedule anything, or bind the organization.

Blank means unresolved. The model never converts a missing owner, budget, venue, operator, partner, consent, or stop condition into implied readiness.
