    Non-negotiable

    Three rules keep the model useful.

    01

    Authority stays named.

    Evidence and preparation do not grant access, spend, work, publication, or representation.

    02

    Assistance never becomes punishment.

    No risk score, eligibility grade, enforcement profile, resident profile, or adverse standing.

    03

    Continuity survives people.

    Sources, decisions, holds, receipts, and handoffs remain legible after the original person leaves.
