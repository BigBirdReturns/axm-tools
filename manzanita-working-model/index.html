    Three safeguards

    Three rules keep help accountable.

    01

    The decision-maker stays named.

    Evidence and preparation do not grant access, spending, work, publication, or representation.

    02

    Evidence gathered to help cannot punish.

    No risk score, eligibility grade, enforcement profile, resident profile, or adverse standing.

    03

    The work survives a handoff.

    Sources, decisions, holds, receipts, and next steps remain legible after the original person leaves.
